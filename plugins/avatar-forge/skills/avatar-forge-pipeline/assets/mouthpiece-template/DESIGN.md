## Style Prompt

Light technical editorial: a porcelain canvas, fine blue drafting lines, restrained ultraviolet accents, and an asymmetric vertical-video layout. The masked speaker remains the primary focal point while captions and compact signal labels create a polished commercial mouthpiece frame.

## Colors

- Canvas: `#F3F7FA`
- Primary ink: `#101C2B`
- Secondary ink: `#4D6074`
- Electric blue: `#076FD8`
- Ultraviolet: `#7257E8`

## Typography

- Chinese and display: `IBM Plex Sans SC`, weights 300 and 700
- Labels and timing: `IBM Plex Mono`, weights 400 and 600

## What NOT to Do

- No dark gamer-style canvas.
- No neon glow around the speaker.
- No equal card grid.
- No overlays covering the speaker's face.
- No motion that competes with the spoken captions.
